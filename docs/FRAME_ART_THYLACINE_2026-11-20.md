The thylacine is extinct. The last known captive animal died in Hobart on 7 September 1936, and Tasmania’s wildlife service reports no conclusive evidence of a living animal since then. Museum specimens, historical photographs and film survive. The National Museum of Australia preserves a complete wet specimen. These pictures reconstruct an animal alive in an imagined Tasmanian woodland. They do not depict a modern sighting or an identified individual.
