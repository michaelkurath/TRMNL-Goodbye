# The Space Shuttle on TRMNL Frame

Verified 26 November 2026.

NASA ended Shuttle operations when STS-135 landed on 21 July 2011. What disappeared was the flight programme, not all of its hardware: Atlantis, Discovery and Endeavour survived retirement, and Enterprise survives as a test orbiter that never flew in space. Columbia and Challenger were lost in flight. NASA recordation preserves engineering history and drawings; its orbiter page includes old future-tense museum relocation wording and is not used as a current visitor-access guide. These images are original artistic reconstructions of a generic orbiter in historical orbital flight, not a live mission or a photograph of a named orbiter. Closed payload doors, thermal protection, double-delta wings and an Earth backdrop establish the subject; tile patterns, perspective, clouds, lighting and minor geometry remain interpretive.
