# The Golden Toad on TRMNL Frame

Verified 27 November 2026.

The golden toad (Incilius periglenes) is extinct. Oxford’s museum account describes the final 1989 observation and the orange males, while distinguishing the dark, spotted females. The last sighting is not an observed death date of the final animal. What survives includes preserved specimens and research records: UT Austin’s DigiMorph documents Texas Natural History Collections specimen TNHC 39447, collected near Monteverde on 29 April 1971 and CT-scanned in 2011. A preserved specimen and its scans are not a surviving living population. The exact causes of the collapse remain debated; no single cause is claimed as settled here. These artworks reconstruct a generic living adult male in imagined Monteverde cloud forest. Colour, skin, anatomy, vegetation, lighting and setting remain interpretive, not an identified animal or an archival photograph. Two initial coarse-texture drafts were rejected; the prompts below describe the accepted independently generated pair.
