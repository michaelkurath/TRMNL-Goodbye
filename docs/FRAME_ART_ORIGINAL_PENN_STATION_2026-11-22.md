# The Original Penn Station on TRMNL Frame

Verified 22 November 2026.

The original above-ground Pennsylvania Station was demolished beginning on 28 October 1963 and continuing through 1966. Its monumental waiting room and concourse disappeared; railway operation continued below ground. PBS documents continued use of the original tunnels, tracks and platforms, while Amtrak’s 2024 briefing describes the station rebuilt below Madison Square Garden and its continuing rail service. Museum photographs and surviving architectural fragments also preserve its memory. These illustrations reconstruct the classical main waiting room before demolition, not the distinct iron-and-glass concourse, current Penn Station or Moynihan Train Hall. Stone colours, furniture, passengers, lighting and architectural details are interpretive, not a measured restoration.
