# The Dodo on TRMNL Frame

Verified 25 November 2026.

The dodo is extinct. NHM places the last reliable sightings in the later seventeenth century, with 1662 commonly cited and possible later dates discussed; that is not an observed death date of the final bird. Subfossil bones, historical descriptions and surviving Oxford specimen material remain. Oxford’s own specimen record documents head and foot material including surviving soft tissue; modern mounted museum birds are reconstructions, not complete original taxidermy specimens. These artworks reconstruct a living bird in an imagined Mauritius habitat. Its upright, comparatively lean appearance follows modern anatomical interpretation; plumage colour, soft parts, vegetation and setting remain interpretive, not a portrait of an identified individual.
