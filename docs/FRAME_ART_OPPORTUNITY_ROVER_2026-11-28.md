What disappeared was the active mission and communications, not a spacecraft returned to Earth: NASA’s final traverse map places the rover at Perseverance Valley on the rim of Endeavour Crater. Its scientific record, images and mapped route remain archived. The final panorama preserves historical views of tracks; no claim is made about their visibility or the rover’s mechanical condition in November 2026.
