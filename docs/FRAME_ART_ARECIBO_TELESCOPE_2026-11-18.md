# The Arecibo Telescope on TRMNL Frame

Verified 18 November 2026.

The 305-metre reflector telescope operated from 1963 until 1 December 2020, when cable failures dropped the instrument platform onto the dish. The giant telescope was lost. The wider observatory site survived and now hosts the NSF Arecibo C3 science education centre. The artwork depicts the intact installation with its three support towers and suspended receiving platform. The slopes and light are interpretive.
