# Frame artwork

Concorde and Kodachrome are original generated illustrations in two separately composed orientations. The exhibits reuse their catalogue text, and their Frame assets are colour JPEGs at 2560 × 1440 and 1440 × 2560. The upper-right corner has room for the title or caption card. Exhibits without Frame art use the existing grayscale asset fitted to preserve the whole subject.

- **Concorde** shows the retired civil airliner, with the ogival delta wing and articulated nose. [Smithsonian National Air and Space Museum](https://airandspace.si.edu/collection-objects/aerospatiale-bac-concorde-fox-alpha-air-france/nasm_A20030139000)
- **Kodachrome** interprets two mounted 35 mm slides in a tabletop still life. [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/kodak-retires-iconic-kodachrome-film)
